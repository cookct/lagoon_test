**The all-nighter (retrospective):** Adam and Simon spent the entire night before the council meeting war-gaming the case in the bedroom — Adam taking Adderall, Simon requesting two of Adam's pills (his own script empty). They identified the statutory basis that dismantled the local authority's position (Section 20(8) — parental right to withdraw consent and reclaim the child at any time; the unlawful foster-to-adopt placement; defective informed consent), built two evidence folders, and planned Adam's "play-dumb" interrogation strategy. Adam tasked Simon with gently extracting the referral source from Maisey (identified as Linda Barrow, a fellow shelter resident).

**The council meeting:** Adam entered posing as a confused American support person, spelling words wrong and mispronouncing statutes to be underestimated. Through escalating "innocent" questions he got Janet Hartley to confirm on record (and on covert phone recording) every procedural failure: no pre-removal observation, no Section 47 investigation, no legal advice offered, consent obtained under duress, and — after speaking privately with the prospective adopters in the waiting room and confirming they had Winnie and were there that morning to finalize — the unlawful fostering-for-adoption placement. He then dropped the friendly mask, laid out the statutory violations, and forced Maisey's formal withdrawal of Section 20 consent. Council solicitor Patricia Newell was called, challenged Adam's standing, then researched him, discovered his undefeated pro se litigation history, and capitulated. Terms secured: suspension of adoption proceedings, contact visit that afternoon, home assessment, full return of Winnie within 72 hours, independent review, apology, and compensation. Adam extracted the referral source from Janet, then revealed he intended to retain outside counsel for Maisey and pursue full financial damages — closing with "Your department is going to raise Winnie after all. Monetarily speaking."

**The contact visit:** At a neutral contact centre, Maisey was reunited with Winnie (who said "Mama"). Adam declined to enter, stating it was hers, not his. He intercepted the grieving prospective adopters, gave them copies of all his documentation, told them they too had been lied to by the council, and proposed a joint lawsuit against the authority.

**The collapse:** Regrouped at Kelly's flat with Nathan, Curtis, Alisha, and Simon, Adam played the meeting recording. Curtis's power triggered — he experienced a timeline in which Adam slid off the sofa in cardiac arrest, was worked on by paramedics, and was pronounced dead at 19:47. Time rewound; Curtis retained the memory. He immediately ordered Kelly and Nathan to call 999 and physically forced Adam downstairs and outside. The ambulance arrived to find Adam apparently healthy on the kerb; moments later Adam dropped into cardiac arrest. Paramedics achieved return of rhythm and transported him to Royal Derby Hospital. Simon attributed the arrest to stimulant use, sleep deprivation, and sustained high-adrenaline cognitive output exceeding Adam's tolerance.

**The hospital wedding:** From his bed, barely able to speak, Adam stole a nurse's phone and called Simon, demanding a marriage license "make it legal, now." Curtis deduced Adam believed he was dying and wanted Kelly legally protected (houses, money, business) — a fiancée inherits nothing. Simon obtained a Registrar General's emergency license (valid where a party is seriously ill and not expected to recover, with medical statement and no notice period); Curtis secured the medical statement and capacity confirmation; Nathan bought two £3.99 rings from a Tesco carousel; Alisha sourced a clean white blouse from a sympathetic nurse. Duty registrar Sandra arrived in nightwear and conducted the ceremony in the cardiac care unit. Kelly and Adam were legally married; both signed, Adam's signature a barely-legible line.

**The second arrest and revival:** Immediately post-ceremony Adam's heart stopped again, the monitor flatlining. Seeing the nurse's face shift into bereavement mode, Kelly punched Adam full-force in the face and screamed at him not to leave her — and the monitor resumed sinus rhythm. Nurses physically restrained and removed her as the crash team arrived to find a living patient. Adam was left sedated, stable but with an unsteady rhythm, and a fresh cheekbone bruise the staff refused to formally document.

**The waiting room:** Removed to the waiting room, the group was joined by Maisey (arriving from Nathan's house in pyjamas). Nathan, Curtis, and Simon recounted the full day to her — the recording, Curtis's death-vision and rewind, the kerbside collapse, the emergency marriage, the Tesco rings, and Kelly's punch-revival. Maisey, briefly guilt-stricken, was told the collapse was Adam's own doing (his inability to stop). She confirmed the contact visit had gone well (Winnie said "Mama"), then comforted Kelly by returning Adam's own words — "they will," "he's gonna be alright" — and took her uninjured hand.

## EMOTIONAL & RELATIONAL STATE

This stretch inverts the arc from triumph to mortal terror, then reforges the unit through crisis. Adam's competence reaches its apex (dismantling a government department in a morning) and immediately collapses into his central flaw made lethal — a brain with no off-switch running his body to death. His neurotype, previously framed as gift and burden, is now shown as potentially fatal. The marriage, legally motivated and executed under deathbed conditions, crystallizes his defining trait: even dying, he thinks only of protecting others (Kelly's legal security). Kelly's arc completes another turn — from the woman terrified Adam would leave, to the woman who physically refuses to let him, punching death back on her wedding night. Curtis is elevated from peripheral cohort member to the reason Adam is alive, his previously minor power now load-bearing. Maisey, hours removed from utter destitution, becomes a source of comfort rather than need, mirroring Adam's own certainty back to a shattered Kelly — the "collected" now doing the collecting. The found-family logic holds under maximum pressure: every member contributes to the emergency (licenses, statements, rings, clothes, revival), and no one leaves the waiting room.

## SEXUAL/PHYSICAL CONTENT

None. This stretch is dominated by medical crisis, legal action, and grief. Physical content is limited to violence in service of survival (Kelly's revival punch) and restraint (nurses removing her).

## SCENE END

The group is assembled in the Royal Derby Hospital waiting room after midnight — Kelly, Nathan, Curtis, Alisha, Simon, and Maisey. Adam is sedated in cardiac care, alive but with an unstable rhythm, his condition unresolved. Kelly's right hand is swelling and split from the punch that restarted his heart; her £3.99 wedding ring is held on by a closed fist. Maisey holds her uninjured hand and reassures her with Adam's own words. No one has left.
