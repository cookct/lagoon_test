## [gotcha] Bond forms unknowingly during teaching
confidence: 0.95
Vhor'ketha (the pouring-through) requires a First One to dissolve their boundary first, but does NOT require either party to name what they're doing. The nine months of Old Tongue lessons were the bond forming - Pim attacks Hermione, mortally wounds her, and only realizes when she feels the scar forming.

## [decision] Pim must be freed first, not last
confidence: 0.9
Pim invented the liberation theory and waited 2000 years. Dramatic logic demands Pim go first, not be freed last 'by choice' overseeing Hogwarts. The original ordering (Aen-Vhar first, Pim last) is narratively backwards.

## [pattern] Original works, fanfic doesn't - why
confidence: 0.85
The original succeeds because Vothrae is a stranger: unknown interior, locked name, history is rumor. The fanfic cannot replicate this because readers already know Hermione - the recontextualization into Potterverse fails because the original is doing something structurally incompatible with established characters.

## [architecture] Smoothest-scar as structural inversion
confidence: 0.9
The 'smoothest-scar' idea - the monster who gets statues and dies in his bed - is the structural inversion that makes the wizarding world's moral economy legible as crime rather than tragedy. This is load-bearing for the book's moral argument.
---

## [context] Pim's 2000 years of slavery
confidence: 0.85
Pim has been bowing to children, cleaning vomit at the Yule Ball, folding robes of boys who became Voldemort's followers. Two thousand years of service. When freed, Pim looks at Hermione and sees the antagonist - the rage builds until an attack that Hermione cannot defend against.
